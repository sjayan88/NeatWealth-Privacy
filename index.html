Privacy Policy

NeatWealth is designed with privacy as its core principle. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. All financial information stays only on your device.

We do not collect

  • Personal identification information
  • Financial data or transaction history
  • Device identifiers or usage analytics
  • Location or contact information

Data storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Android's local database. It is never sent to any server, cloud service or third party.

How mileage is calculated

Mileage (fuel efficiency) is calculated using the fill-up segment method. Each segment's efficiency is computed as: distance travelled ÷ litres filled at the next fill-up. The average mileage shown is the mean of all individual segment values. The first fill-up only establishes the starting odometer and its fuel quantity is excluded from calculations. Readings outside a realistic range (2–100 km/L) are excluded automatically. Results are indicative — actual efficiency depends on driving conditions, load, and other factors.

Internet usage

NeatWealth works entirely offline. It connects to the internet only when you use the optional currency converter, which fetches exchange rates from a public exchange-rate API (exchangerate-api.com). Only a currency code (for example "INR") is sent — no personal or financial data is transmitted.

Backups and exports

Any backup (.db) or CSV file you create is saved on your device and shared only where you choose to send it (for example your own Google Drive, email or messaging app). NeatWealth never uploads these files automatically.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the security of your device, your app PIN or biometric lock, and any backup or exported files you create. The developer is not responsible for data loss or unauthorised access to exported files once they leave the app.

Children's privacy

As no data is collected from any user, the app is safe for users of all ages.
